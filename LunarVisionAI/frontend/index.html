CHANDRAYAAN-2 OHRC • SIH1732 DEEP DIVE

Core Technical Architecture

Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs) at the lunar poles have remained in perpetual darkness for over 2 billion years. Sunlight never penetrates directly—only faint secondary photons scattered from surrounding crater rims illuminate the floor. Here is how our 4-stage engineering pipeline solves this:

☼
PILLAR 01

Low-Light Image Enhancement

The Challenge: PSR interiors receive less than 1% of direct lunar illumination, resulting in extremely low Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) and severe photon starvation.

✦Percentile Radiometric Stretch: Dynamically remaps faint scattered photon data between 1st and 99.5th percentiles without washing out sunlit crater walls.
✦Edge-Preserving Bilateral Denoising: Eliminates sensor Poisson-Gaussian noise while preserving microscopic crater rims and boulder boundaries.
◇
PILLAR 02

Deep Learning & Computer Vision

The Challenge: There are zero ground-truth daylight photographs of lunar PSR craters to train traditional supervised neural networks.

✦Zero-Reference Deep Curve Estimation (Zero-DCE): Deep convolutional network that iteratively learns pixel-wise non-linear exposure curves without needing paired ground truth images.
✦Anti-Hallucination Guard: Strict gradient fidelity checks ensure no artificial boulders or fake craters are hallucinated by the AI.
🚀
PILLAR 03

Satellite Imagery & Remote Sensing

The Challenge: Chandrayaan-2's Orbiter High Resolution Camera (OHRC) captures ultra-high spatial resolution images (0.25 m/pixel), generating massive gigapixel strips.

✦High-Res Patch Tiling: Processes massive orbital swath strips in sliding-window tiles with seam-blending to prevent browser memory exhaustion.
✦Lunar Polar Stereographic Projection: Preserves exact coordinates (Latitude/Longitude) and scale for rover traverse and lander hazard safety analysis.
🔭
PILLAR 04

Astronomical Image Processing

The Challenge: Space radiation bombards sensor arrays with high-energy cosmic rays, creating false hot-pixel artifacts that resemble small rocks.

✦Cosmic Ray & Hot-Pixel Despeckling: Astronomical median sigma-clipping scrubs radiation spikes from raw detector readouts.
✦Photometric I/F Albedo Mapping: Calculates lunar regolith reflectance factor and reveals potential cold-trap water-ice deposits via false-color thermal spectrum.

🪐 Mission Impact: Artemis & Chandrayaan Exploration

Permanently Shadowed Regions host volatile water-ice reservoirs in ultra-cold traps (<40 K). By turning pitch-black shadows into actionable scientific terrain maps, our system enables ISRO and global space agencies to safely locate water resources, verify rover driveability, and identify safe landing zones for future lunar missions.
